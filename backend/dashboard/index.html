              Connected mobile devices

              Connect as a mobile device?

              Only approve this if the same code is shown in the LeakTrace app on your device right now. For up to 30 days the device will be able to read your websites and aliases, email activity and timeline, and analyze, save and manage SMS investigations; you can revoke it here at any time.

                Privacy

                Records you create are visible only to your account. Records from before accounts existed are not linked to anyone and are not shown here. LeakTrace sends no analytics.

                Delete account

                This permanently deletes your account and everything it owns: websites, aliases, consent receipts, received-email records and investigations. It cannot be undone.

                New alias

                LeakTrace generates an alias and a nickname to use when you sign up for this website. Your real name is never asked for or stored.
