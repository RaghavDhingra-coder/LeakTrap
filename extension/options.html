LeakTrace extension settings

Development setting. This version only talks to a LeakTrace backend on this computer (http://127.0.0.1). The backend has no user authentication, so this is not a production-ready setup. Whatever database that backend uses receives the records you create.

Only http://127.0.0.1:<port> is accepted. Nothing is sent until you act in the popup.

LeakTrace account

Not connected.

Connecting opens your LeakTrace dashboard, where you approve this extension while signed in. The extension never sees your password. Its access is limited to creating and looking up your aliases and recording consent receipts, expires after a few hours, and is kept in memory only (connect again after restarting the browser). You can revoke it from the dashboard.

Autofill

Only runs on the tab where you open the LeakTrace popup. Every fill needs your click; forms are never submitted.

Sites where you chose “Never”

    What this extension stores

    • These settings (the backend URL, whether autofill is on, and the hostnames where you chose “Never”), in local extension storage.
    • The signup you last created or selected (ID, site, alias, nickname), in session storage, which is kept in memory and cleared when the browser closes. Use “Forget” in the popup to clear it sooner.
    • Your account connection (a short-lived, revocable token and your email address), in session storage only. Use “Disconnect” to remove it.
    • Nothing else: no browsing history, page content, form values, passwords, cookies or receipt text. Aliases are looked up from the backend when you ask, not saved per site.
